Mustafa Çakı

CEO · Vertex

Moving constantly with my teacher father, I witnessed extreme poverty; friends lost to the streets and families denied healthcare. My motivation is eradicating this suffering. A century after Atatürk began uplifting Türkiye, his mission remains unfinished.

My ultimate goal is global Eudaimonia, true human prosperity. I chose technology because it offers the fastest way to scale meaningful impact.

Despite dozens of rejections and legal hurdles as high schoolers, we built Cortex and organically reached 100,000+ global users without marketing spend.

Over three years at Vertex, I've led thousands of people through hundreds of challenges. I believe everyone carries a hidden spark, and my greatest strength is turning those sparks into collective fire.

I missed Teknopark High School by two points. Unable to enter as a student, I founded a startup and returned as a CEO. Three years later, Vertex entered Teknopark's incubation. One door closed, so I kicked down another.

In ten years, I intend to lead one of the world's largest companies, using technology to uplift Türkiye and advance prosperity worldwide. To me, this is already written destiny; my only job is to live it out.
